Events

30th Oct 2025

Haldi Ceremony Ammachiveedu, Kottayam

During the ceremony, family members apply a mixture of turmeric (haldi), oil, and water to the bride and groom. The mixture is believed to bless the couple and ward off evil spirits.

Mehndi & Sangeet

The Mehndi ceremony involves applying intricate henna designs to the bride, symbolizing good luck. It's often paired with the Sangeet, a festive celebration of singing, music, and dancing where both families mingle and enjoy the pre-wedding excitement.

3rd Nov 2025

Wedding Sri Guruvayoor temple

Wedding day taking place during the auspicious time of the morning. A key ritual is the 'Thalikettu,' where the groom ties a sacred gold necklace (thaali) around the bride's neck, symbolizing their marital union.

4th Nov 2025

Reception Stay Samara Heritage Villa

The wedding reception is the joyous celebration held immediately following the marriage ceremony. It is essentially a party where the newlyweds are formally received by society, allowing guests to mingle, enjoy food, drinks, music, and dancing.
